The Hallery Studio Team is made up of young, innovative, dynamic, and extremely driven individuals that are passionate about transforming concepts into creative designs and materialized these designs into real end product.

Who Are We?

Established in 2020, Hallery Studio is a national firm of architects and interior designers founded on the belief that ideas and skilled craftsmanship are essential in enriching people's lives. Our studio is based on Sleman - Yogyakarta with experiences designing projects that spans across Indonesia, focus exclusively on providing finest quality, excellent service, punctuality and partnership during the entire project.

What We Offer

We pay attention to our customers, comprehend their demands, and address them.


Our method involves the strategic integration of technical disciplines, regardless of the project's nature—renovation, construction, or rejuvenation.


Our goals are the best quality, the quickest turnaround time, and the best financial value.


We demand top-notch finishing and place a high priority on project management and meticulous execution.
